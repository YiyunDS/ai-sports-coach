See good skating form in 3D. Spin it around and look from every side!

What to look for

  • Knees bent. The blue slice at the knee shows the knee angle. Smaller number = more bend = more power.
  • Lean forward a little. The slice at the hips shows how far the back tips forward.
  • Stick on the ice. The blade stays down, ready for a pass.
  • Push all the way. Press Show the stride and watch the pushing leg get almost straight.
